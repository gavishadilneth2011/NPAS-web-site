01 — About NPAS

More than photography.A culture of seeing.

Founded in 1950, NPAS grew from a handful of students sharing a single camera into one of the most respected student photographic societies in the region. A great photograph begins long before the shutter — in curiosity, patience, and the discipline of truly looking.

04 — Instructor

Mr.Upananda Senavirathna

“I don't teach students to press a button. I teach them to wait — for the light, for the gesture, for the moment the world arranges itself.”

A documentary photographer with over two decades in the field, Mr. Varma has guided NPAS members to national exhibitions and shaped the society's critique-driven approach to learning.
